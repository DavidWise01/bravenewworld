Declared courage is not observed action.

John, Helmholtz and Bernard share discontent with the World State but respond differently when soma distribution becomes a public confrontation. Action, intent, consequences and risk must remain distinct records.

Three witnesses, different behavior

John: publicly challenges soma distribution, seizes rations and confronts the crowd. Helmholtz: chooses to support him amid risk. Bernard: hesitates and avoids the immediate conflict. These are behavioral observations, not complete verdicts on any character.
