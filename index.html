The story begins

A group of students follows a proud Director through machines designed to turn births into schedules, people into castes, and uncertainty into a managed output. Every vessel has a number. Every category has a purpose. But what is missing from the tour?

Explore the seven acts, inspect the source record, and choose how to interpret each scene. Your choices do not rewrite the novel; they help you examine its central conflict.

How the project grows

Each chapter is a self-contained playable story with original pixel animation, accessible prose, dialogue, observation records, and a thematic witness crosswalk. This new repository is being published chapter by chapter, beginning with B001 through B004; later chapters are mapped in the atlas but are not misrepresented as released.
