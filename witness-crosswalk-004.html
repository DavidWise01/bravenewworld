Shared discomfort is not shared causation.

Chapter Four introduces two men who feel alienated from the same world for different reasons. Bernard Marx is wounded by status anxiety and exclusion; Helmholtz Watson has recognition but finds his creative gifts underused. Their friendship is a genuine meeting, not a merged biography.

Track A · Bernard's exclusion

Bernard feels publicly embarrassed and socially small; he refuses an offer of soma but then treats lower-caste workers harshly. Being diminished does not automatically generate empathy.
