Popularity is not a promise of lasting friendship.

Bernard's invited guests expect to meet John, but John refuses. Their disappointment costs Bernard the popularity he gained from John's celebrity. Helmholtz's poetry and friendship with John offer a contrast: companionship grounded in chosen exchange, not a crowd's expectations.

Borrowed popularity / party guests

Guests came for John, not Bernard. Their departure reveals the conditions attached to the attention Bernard enjoyed.
